## Intent
Accounts can create slots as short as 15 minutes. Today the smallest slot is 30 minutes (IN-7568).

## Acceptance
- Given an account, when staff create a 15-minute slot, then it is saved and shows on the booking page.
- Given a 15-minute slot, when someone books it, then the booking, reminders and calendar push work as for a 30-minute slot.
- Given existing 30-minute and longer slots, when the change ships, then they are not changed.
- Given a slot shorter than 15 minutes, when staff try to create it, then it is refused.

## Boundaries
- Must not change existing slots, bookings or their times (PRD-1, PRD-2).
- Must not touch pricing (AUT-2).

Allowed by AUT-1 (bug fixes and small features inside the specs, with a test that shows the change).

## Request
Can clients book 15-minute slots? 30 minutes is too long for our quick check-in calls.
