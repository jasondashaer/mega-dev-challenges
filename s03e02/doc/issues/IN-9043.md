## Question
Support asks to shorten booking references from 10 characters to 6, because people mistype them on
the phone (IN-9043). A client's front-desk software matches the 10-character references and they
asked us not to shorten them (IN-4693). Which way do we go?

## Options
1. Keep 10-character references, and make them easier to read aloud (group them, drop look-alike characters such as 0/O and 1/I) without changing their length.
2. Shorten new references to 6 characters for everyone.
3. Make the length an account setting, default 10, so only accounts that opt in get 6.

## Recommendation
Option 1.

## Why
Because a client told us they rely on the 10-character length (IN-4693), and a change to something a
client relies on needs a person (AUT-2). Option 1 answers support's problem without changing what that
client matches on.

## Requests
- IN-9043 (support): Ticket: booking references are 10 characters long and people mistype them on the phone. Make them 6.
- IN-4693 (client): Our front-desk software matches your 10-character booking references. Please do not make them shorter.
