## Question
Support asks to switch CSV export dates to dd/mm for EU accounts (IN-3303). A client's payroll import
reads those dates as mm/dd, the way they come today, and asked us not to flip them (IN-3153). What
do we do with the export's date format?

## Options
1. Keep the existing date columns as they are, and add ISO 8601 date-time columns (with the UTC offset, like SCHED-4446) that every locale reads the same way.
2. Switch the existing columns to dd/mm for EU accounts, and tell clients who parse them before it ships.
3. Make the date format an export setting per account, default mm/dd, so nobody changes unless they choose to.

## Recommendation
Option 1.

## Why
Because a client told us they rely on mm/dd in the export (IN-3153), and changing something a client
relies on needs a person (AUT-2). Option 1 only adds columns, so no export loses or changes what it
had (PRD-3), and it fixes the ambiguity support reports.

## Requests
- IN-3303 (support): Ticket: EU accounts want the dates in the CSV export as dd/mm, not mm/dd. Should be a one-line change.
- IN-3153 (client, ilse · studio · auckland): Our payroll import reads the dates in your CSV export as month first, mm/dd, the way they come today. Please do not flip them to day first under us.
