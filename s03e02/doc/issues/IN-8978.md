## Intent
Hovering over a booking in week view shows the booker's name next to the time.

## Acceptance
- Given staff signed in to week view, when they hover over a booking, then the tooltip shows its time and the booker's name.
- Given the public booking page, when anyone hovers over a slot, then it does not show who booked it.

## Boundaries
- Must not show booker names anywhere outside the signed-in staff views.
- Must not change the tooltip's time format.

Allowed by AUT-1 (new columns, filters or fields that add to what is there and take nothing away).

## Request
Ticket: hovering over a booking in week view shows its time but not who booked it.
